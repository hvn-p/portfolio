About

Full-stack developer, 25, with close to five years on SaaS products in production. An application starts from what its users need to do, then from an architecture that holds over time, and AI runs through both. What I am looking for: a product to watch grow, at a scale that demands better design.

What I do

  • Full stackReact, Next.js and Vue, NestJS, GraphQL and tRPC, PostgreSQL and MongoDB, Docker, Kubernetes, Azure, infrastructure as code with Pulumi and Bicep.
  • AI programmingAI writes most of the code; what makes it reliable is its harness: instructions, skills, the data it can reach, validation of what it produces. I build that harness and choose the architecture the problem calls for. Spec-driven development with GitHub SpecKit, agentic coding with Claude Code, several tasks in parallel.
